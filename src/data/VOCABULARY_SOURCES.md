# Vocabulary data sources

The 1,500-word dataset combines and deduplicates two openly available academic-vocabulary sources. Category assignments, Japanese labels, learning levels, cloze masks, and collocation snippets are adapted for Mylingo.

- [TOEFL Essential Vocabulary Dataset](https://github.com/gungorkaya-eng/toefl-essential-vocabulary-dataset) — 1,000 entries with part of speech, academic theme, definition, and example sentence. MIT License. The upstream author requests attribution to [wordlevel.net](https://wordlevel.net/).
- [New Academic Word List resources](https://jodiewilliamcampbelleslefl.weebly.com/the-new-academic-word-list-nawl.html) — headwords and English–Japanese definitions based on NAWL 1.01.

This is an academically useful study selection. It is not presented as an official ETS frequency ranking or an ETS publication.

## Complete the Words shortlist

Complete the Words is limited to 150 words selected for active use in TOEFL Writing. The shortlist favors reusable language for argumentation, comparison, cause and effect, evaluation, evidence, synthesis, and discussion of sources; narrow subject-matter terms are excluded. The selection is informed by the official [TOEFL iBT Writing section description](https://www.ets.org/toefl/test-takers/ibt/about/content/writing/) and [Writing rubrics](https://www.ets.org/content/dam/ets-org/pdfs/toefl/toefl-ibt-writing-rubrics.pdf), together with the corpus-based [Academic Word List](https://www.wgtn.ac.nz/lals/resources/academicwordlist).
